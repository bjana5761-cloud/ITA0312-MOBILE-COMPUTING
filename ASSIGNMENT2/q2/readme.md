# Q2 — FDMA vs TDMA

A cell has 2 MHz per direction. FDMA: 25 kHz channels gives 80 nominal channels. TDMA: 200 kHz carriers × 8 time slots gives 10 × 8 = 80 nominal channels.

After the stated guard/control overhead, the report estimates 75 usable FDMA traffic channels and 70 usable TDMA traffic channels. The conclusion is that equal nominal channel count does not imply equal usable capacity.
